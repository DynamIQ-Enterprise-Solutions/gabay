## 1. What this spec covers

Invariant 10: "Every position is inside the venue boundary, which covers the whole property (L30), or it is declared outside." On the phone this needs:

1. **The boundary on the phone.** The app reads the boundary from the held package. P0-03 already ships it there (its allow-list: "venue (code, name, boundary, georeference, …)") and refuses a publish with any element outside it (P0-03 AC-16). This spec adds no server work.
2. **The guard** (`lib/core/positioning/`, the Boundary rule row). Every position from every position source is tested against the open mall's boundary before anything draws it, snaps it or routes from it.
3. **The out-of-venue state.** What the map, the place sheet and an active route do while the position is outside.
4. **The route-start guard.** Go is disabled while outside, and the app says why (PRD §6.10).
5. **The offer to switch malls** on "a selection from another venue", read as a position inside another mall (E1, a G4 reinterpretation after L107).
6. **A way to see it on this machine:** "Put me here", added by FF-8 to the internal "Test position" tool (A16). No real position source in FF-8 can put a shopper outside a Demo Malls venue (§2, §5).

## 2. Settled context

- **E-20 (L153):** localhost only, no deploy, throwaway data. This spec adds no route and no admin screen, so it adds no retrofit-ledger row.
- **Where the boundary comes from (B2):** until FF-10 draws it, `Venue.BoundaryGeoJson` is a server-made rectangle from (0, 0) to the largest level's `WidthM` × `DepthM` (P0-01 AC-34). The guard must work unchanged once FF-10 draws a real polygon.
- **The frame (invariant 2; E10):** positions are local planar metres in one frame per venue. The boundary is in the same frame. The schema holds one boundary per venue, not one per level, so a position is judged on its x and y whatever its level (outdoor ground and basements included).
- **On the line (P0-03 DC-12, accepted D15):** a point on the boundary line counts as inside. The guard uses the same rule, so whatever publish accepted as inside is also inside on the phone.
- **One position source, six states (A8):** P0-08 owns one `PositionSource` with six confidence states: `none`, `approximate`, `handSet`, `liveConfident`, `liveUnsure` (grey is `liveUnsure` with a grey flag) and `guided` (P0-08 §2). Being outside the venue is a separate boundary status (unknown, inside, outside), not a seventh state.
- **Producers by FF-8:**
  - P0-08 (FF-6): a place picked in "Where are you?", placed at its door (publish proved every door inside, P0-03 AC-16); a floor pick (`approximate`) that a tap refines (`handSet`).
  - P0-15 (FF-8, this slice): the guided dot (`guided`), which moves only along a route and so is always inside.
  - P0-04's internal "Start here": removed by P0-08 (A1).
  - The internal "Test position" tool (A16): FF-7 builds it with "Walk the route" (P0-14); FF-8 adds "Put me here" (AC-31). Like "Walk the route", "Put me here" is the position source while switched on, and the takeover rule below does not apply to it (R9). Release builds have neither.
  - The live dot (BLE, and GNSS outdoors through the georeference, L31) is P0-13 (FF-9). It is the first real source that can report a position outside.
- **Takeover (A8, R6):** a `liveConfident` fix takes over a `handSet` position only if it is inside the boundary and comes after P0-13 has re-seeded its estimate at the hand-set spot; it takes over an `approximate` position if it is inside the boundary, with no re-seed (R6). `liveUnsure` is a route origin and grey is not; a held hand-set position beats a `liveUnsure` one. During a guided walk a live fix is offered, never taken (E16). This spec owns the "inside the boundary" part; the re-seed is P0-13's.
- **A refining tap beyond the boundary (A24):** from FF-8 it is refused at the tap (AC-30). In FF-6, before the guard exists, P0-08 EC-15's rule stands: the point is set and routing refuses it as a start.
- **The Demo Malls (§G ruling 1):** no beacons, no georeference, run on the fallback. From FF-8 nothing there can report a position outside except "Put me here" (A16, AC-31), because a refining tap beyond the boundary is refused (A24).
- **Detection (P0-04 AC-14, AC-17):** the picker's "You're here" is P0-04's and is unchanged. A mall without a georeference is never detected.
- **Wording (L113; E2):** "places and surroundings are always worded suggestively and actions plainly." PRD §6.10's "You're outside ⟨venue⟩" becomes "You seem to be outside ⟨venue⟩", recorded as a G4 difference in FF-8's L-row (§L).
- **Go with no position (C2):** from FF-6, Go with no position stays enabled and opens "Where are you?" (P0-04 r3's AC-56). Being outside is not "no position" for Go: PRD §6.10 disables starting a route while outside and says why (AC-19).
- **Spoken lines (A14):** P0-14 (FF-7) words every spoken sentence and owns the voice queue, both channels and the muted state (D2). This spec only says when a line is spoken.
- **The turning map (A15):** P0-04 r3 builds "Map faces the way I walk" in FF-7, so it exists when FF-8 holds it (AC-22).
- **Tagalog (A2; §M 3):** P0-14 lands in FF-7, before FF-8, and FF-8 has no Tagalog waiver. flutter-coder drafts each new phrase's Tagalog; the product owner corrects and approves every line through FF-7's review sheet (key, English, Tagalog, where it shows, Tagalog screenshots) in FF-8's pull request, at FF-8's sign-off. Only approved lines merge, and the approval goes in FF-8's L-row. If FF-7 has not merged when FF-8's plan is written, the plan stops and asks.
- **Privacy and foreground (invariants 4, 5):** the boundary decision is made on the phone. Nothing about it is sent anywhere, and nothing runs in the background.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

### A. The boundary on the phone

- AC-1: The guard reads the open mall's boundary from the held package's venue row. It makes no network request, so it works with no connection. Verify: unit (fixture package; an HTTP client that fails if called).
- AC-2: A point (x, y) in the venue's planar metres is inside when it lies within the boundary or on its line (P0-03 DC-12). It is judged on x and y alone, on every level, the outdoor ground and basements included. Inner rings (holes) count as outside, and a MultiPolygon is inside if any of its polygons holds the point (§10 DC-3). Verify: unit (boundary vectors: inside, outside, on an edge, on a vertex, in a concave notch, in a hole, far away).
- AC-3: The phone's inside test and P0-03's containment check give the same answer on one shared file of boundary vectors (§10 DC-1). A point publish accepted as inside is never outside on the phone. Verify: unit (Dart), and unit (Node: a new test that runs P0-03's check over the same file, recorded in P0-03 r3 as FF-8's test addition with no behaviour change, §L; no existing test changes).
- AC-4: B2's derived rectangle needs no special handling. The same guard works on a drawn concave polygon. Verify: unit (Aurora's published rectangle; a concave fixture).

### B. The guard

- AC-5: Every position from every producer of the `PositionSource` passes through one guard in `lib/core/positioning/` before it is drawn, snapped to a walkway, glided or used as a route origin. No consumer reads a producer around it. Verify: unit (a fake source; the snap and the router are never called with an outside position) and review.
- AC-6: A position outside the boundary is never snapped to a walkway, never drawn as a dot, halo or cone, and never handed to routing (PRD §6.10). Verify: unit.
- AC-7: The out-of-venue state is set on the update that first reports a position outside ("within one position cycle", PRD §6.10). It clears on the first update inside. Each reported point decides; no new setting is added; smoothing noisy sources is P0-13's (E3). Verify: unit (a fake source and clock).
- AC-8: The guard publishes a boundary status (unknown before any position, inside, outside) beside the `PositionSource`'s six confidence states (`none`, `approximate`, `handSet`, `liveConfident`, `liveUnsure`, `guided`; A8, Blueprint §4.3). The status is a separate value, never a seventh confidence state (§10 DC-5). The map, the place sheet, the route sheet, P0-14's wording and P0-15 read it (§10 DC-2). Verify: unit.
- AC-9: A position the guard cannot judge (a coordinate that is not a finite number, or a level that is not in the held package) is dropped. It is not drawn, not used, and does not change the status (§10 DC-4). Verify: unit.
- AC-10: The boundary decision never leaves the phone. No request the app makes carries a position, the status or a change of status (invariant 4; G3). Verify: unit (a failing HTTP client while the status changes), the `position-privacy` lint, and the review privacy pass.
- AC-11: The guard runs only while the app is in the foreground. Leaving the app stops it, and on return the next position decides the status afresh (invariant 5). Verify: unit (lifecycle fake).
- AC-12: Changing mall, by Me → "Change mall" or by the offer of §E, clears the position and the status. A position belongs to one venue's map (P0-04 EC-35). The status is never saved across a restart, just as an active route is not restored (PRD §6.4). Verify: widget.

### C. The out-of-venue state on screen (E2)

- AC-13: While outside, the map shows a card at the top: "You seem to be outside ⟨venue⟩", with "Where are you?" (P0-08) and "Change mall" (P0-04 AC-59). No dot is drawn. Recentre is hidden, as with no position (E8). No level chip is marked as the shopper's level. The wording differs from PRD §6.10's "You're outside ⟨venue⟩" by ruling (E2, L113), recorded in FF-8's L-row (G4). Verify: widget, and screenshots in light and dark, phone and tablet widths.
- AC-14: The map stays usable while outside: levels, views, pinch, rotate, the place sheet and search all work. Only what needs the shopper's position is held (AC-19, AC-20). Verify: widget.
- AC-15: Being on the outdoor ground or in an outdoor car park inside the boundary (L30) never shows the out-of-venue state. Its wording cannot be confused with an outdoor line that P0-13 may show. Verify: unit (a position on the outdoor ground level inside the rectangle) and widget.
- AC-16: Entering the state is announced once to a screen reader (a live region). Every new control has a screen-reader name. Nothing overflows at text size ×1.4 on a 320 dp wide phone (G2; PRD §6.23). Verify: widget (semantics; ×1.4 at 320 dp).
- AC-17: Every new colour comes from `gabay_tokens.dart` through the theme, in light and dark (rule 6). Verify: the `colour-literals` lint and screenshots.
- AC-18: Every new shopper phrase is in `app_en.arb` and in the Tagalog ARB from P0-14, and the untranslated-phrase test passes. flutter-coder drafts the Tagalog; each phrase goes on FF-7's review sheet (key, English, Tagalog, where it shows, Tagalog screenshots) in FF-8's pull request; the product owner corrects and approves every line, and only approved lines merge. The approval is recorded in FF-8's L-row (A2; §M 3; L109; G2). Verify: unit (the ARB test) and manual (the owner's review sheet, approved and recorded).

### D. The route-start guard

- AC-19: While outside, Go on every place sheet is disabled with the line "You seem to be outside ⟨venue⟩. Set where you are to get a route." (PRD §6.10: "starting a route is disabled and the app says why"; E2). This differs on purpose from Go with no position, which from FF-6 stays enabled and opens "Where are you?" (C2; P0-04 r3 AC-56). Verify: widget (outside: disabled with the line; no position: enabled, opens the sheet).
- AC-20: While outside, everything else that reads the shopper's position behaves as it does with no position (§10 DC-2): the "nearest" chips (which open "Set where you are to see what's nearest", B1), "About N m to walk", ordering by distance (P0-05's no-origin state) and Recentre. No distance is ever computed from an outside position. Verify: unit (P0-05's distance service is never called with an outside origin) and widget.
- AC-21: While outside, choosing a place in "Where are you?" sets a hand-set position at its door. The status turns inside on that update, the card goes, and Go is enabled again. Verify: widget.
- AC-22: When a route is active and the position goes outside, the trip stays and guidance holds (E4): no direction, voice cue, vibration, off-route count, arrival detection or turning of the map (the turning map is P0-04 r3's, FF-7, A15). The route stays drawn. The route sheet shows the out-of-venue line with "Where are you?". With Voice guidance on and not muted, the line is spoken once, worded and queued by P0-14 (A14, D2). "Find another way" and "Guide me step by step" are disabled with the same line. Back inside, guidance resumes from the new position under the normal off-route rule (L80; off route held while outside is in P0-06 r4, §L). The route never switches by itself (L110). Verify: widget (fake source and router).
- AC-23: During a guided walk (P0-15), a live fix outside the boundary is never offered as "Your position is back. Use it?" (PRD §6.6; E16: the offer is made only when the live dot turns confident, and a live fix is offered, never taken, A8). The guided dot itself is always on the route, so it is always inside. Verify: unit.
- AC-24: Preview's ghost dot (L118) is not a position. It never passes through the guard and never changes the status. Verify: unit.

### E. A selection from another venue (E1)

- AC-25: When the position is outside the open mall and inside exactly one other published mall's boundary, a card offers "You seem to be at ⟨other mall⟩. Switch?" with Switch and Stay. Both malls must carry a georeference, and the other mall's boundary comes from P0-04's public list, so its package need not be held. Nothing is placed on the current map. This reinterprets PRD §6.10's "a selection from another venue" after L107 removed QR, recorded in FF-8's L-row (E1; G4). Verify: unit (fixtures with two georeferenced malls) and widget.
- AC-26: Switch follows P0-04's switch. During a trip it first asks "End your route to ⟨place⟩?" (AC-59). A mall whose package is not held downloads and is verified first (P0-04 AC-13, AC-19). No position is placed on the other mall's map until its package is verified and the next position arrives. Verify: widget.
- AC-27: Stay closes the card, and that mall is not offered again until the app is next opened. Verify: widget.
- AC-28: A mall without a georeference, which includes every Demo Malls venue, is never offered (P0-04 AC-17; §G ruling 1). Two overlapping candidate boundaries offer neither, as P0-04's detection does (E2 of `plan/FF-1-4-decisions.md`). The offer is tested with fixtures until a real venue has a georeference (E1). Verify: unit.

### F. Hand-set positions (P0-08)

- AC-29: A place picked in "Where are you?" passes the guard like any other position. That is defence in depth, because publish already proved its door inside (P0-03 AC-16). Verify: unit.
- AC-30: From FF-8, a tap that refines an approximate dot at a point beyond the boundary is refused at the tap. The approximate dot stays where it was, and a line says "That spot is outside ⟨venue⟩. Tap inside the map." (A24). This replaces P0-08 EC-15's FF-6 behaviour for taps beyond the boundary (the point is set and routing refuses it as a start); P0-08 r2 records the change, and FF-8's plan names the P0-08 EC-15 test it changes (C1). Taps inside the boundary but in a walled room or a void keep P0-08 EC-15's rule. Verify: widget.

### G. Seeing it on this machine (A16)

- AC-31: In internal builds only, the "Test position" tool (built by FF-7 with "Walk the route", P0-14) gains "Put me here". While it is on, a long press anywhere on the map, inside the venue or beyond it, sets the tool's simulated position there in the `liveConfident` state, and that point goes through the guard like any other. While "Put me here" is on, the tool is the producer in use; turning it off removes it (Q2 (a)). Like "Walk the route", it is the position source while switched on: the inside-the-boundary takeover rule (AC-33) does not apply to it, so a long press beyond the boundary shows the out-of-venue state even while a hand-set position was held (R9). The tool sits behind the internal-tools build flag in Blueprint invariant 16's registry (`GABAY_INTERNAL_TOOLS`, P0-04 AC-50). A release build has neither the entry nor its handler (L85). P0-13 decides whether the tool stays (A16). Verify: widget (flag off; flag on: a long press beyond Aurora's rectangle shows the card) and review.

### H. A package whose boundary cannot be used (E5)

- AC-32: If the held package's boundary is unreadable, not a Polygon or MultiPolygon, or has no area, the mall still opens for browsing. Every position is declared outside (invariant 10's "or it is declared outside"). Go is disabled with "This map can't place you yet. Try again after the next update." Internal builds log the reason. The next refresh check (P0-04) may bring a fixed version. Verify: unit (fixture packages: bad JSON, a LineString, a zero-size ring) and widget.

### I. Live fixes against a hand-set position (from FF-9; A8)

- AC-33: While a `handSet` or `approximate` position is held, only a `liveConfident` fix *inside* the boundary may take over (A8; for a hand-set position the further condition, that P0-13 has re-seeded its estimate at the hand-set spot, is P0-13's; an approximate position needs no re-seed, R6). A live fix outside leaves the held position in place and does not show the state. The state shows from live fixes only when the live source is already the one in use. The internal "Put me here" tool is not a live fix and this rule does not apply to it (R9, AC-31). FF-8 builds and tests the boundary part with a fake live source; FF-9 proves it with the real one, and P0-09 is marked `DONE` only after that proof (R10). Verify: unit; manual (FF-9's on-site run, P0-13 AC-55).

## 10. Design choices (accepted 2026-10-12, A7)

- **DC-1: one shared file of boundary vectors, run by the phone's test and by a new Node test of P0-03's check.** This keeps publish and the phone agreeing. *Alternative:* separate tests on each side; simpler, but the two could drift on edge rules such as the on-line rule.
- **DC-2: outside means "no usable position" everywhere a position is read (chips, distances, Recentre), plus an explicit status; Go is disabled with its own line (AC-19).** *Alternative:* keep the last inside position as the origin for distances; this shows numbers the app cannot justify (PRD §6.10's description).
- **DC-3: GeoJSON Polygon and MultiPolygon are read; holes count as outside.** FF-10 may draw a property split by a public road. *Alternative:* Polygon with an outer ring only, and anything else treated as unusable (AC-32). How rings are ordered and closed is RECALLED from RFC 7946 and checked before the plan relies on it.
- **DC-4: a position the guard cannot judge is dropped, not declared outside.** *Alternative:* declare it outside; but that would flash the card on one bad reading.
- **DC-5: the boundary status is a separate value beside the confidence state, not a seventh confidence state.** Confirmed by A8. *Alternative:* add "outside" to Blueprint §4.3's list; every consumer of confidence would then need an extra case.

## 11. RECALLED claims (checked before they are load-bearing in the plan)

- RFC 7946's Polygon rules: the first ring is the outer one, later rings are holes, rings are closed, and the winding order is advisory (DC-3).
- How a self-contained, sideloaded internal APK records the guard's timings into a results file (§5; N12). Whether `integration_test` can do this without a connected host is RECALLED and unchecked; the plan checks it or names another way.

## 12. Questions for the user (all ruled 2026-10-12, L165)

**Q1 [spec-writer] What "a selection from another venue" means now that QR is gone (L107).** The PRD's only selection source was a scanned code. The demo keeps favourites and recents per venue, so no picked place can come from another mall.
- (a) *Recommended.* The offer comes from position: outside the open mall and inside exactly one other georeferenced mall's boundary → "You seem to be at ⟨mall⟩. Switch?" (Switch / Stay; Stay holds until the app is next opened). Built now, verified with fixtures, first seen when a real venue has a georeference. Recorded as a G4 reinterpretation. (AC-25 to AC-28)
- (b) Withdraw the criterion: nothing in the app can select another mall's place since L107. A G4 L-row amends PRD §6.10 and the pipeline entry; AC-25 to AC-27 are dropped.
- (c) A generic guard only: any place or position tagged with another venue's id gets the offer, with no path to it today, kept for later sources.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, E1): (a).** Applied in AC-25 to AC-28.

**Q2 [spec-writer] How you see the out-of-venue state on localhost in FF-8.** No FF-8 source can put a Demo Malls shopper outside.
- (a) *Recommended.* An internal-build "Simulate my position" test tool: a long press anywhere, beyond the venue included, feeds a simulated live-confident position. It sits behind the existing internal-tools flag and is stripped from release builds, and P0-13 decides whether it stays. (AC-31)
- (b) Fixtures only. You first see the state in FF-9, in the penthouse.
- (c) Wire the phone's location channel as a coarse live source now. This needs a georeference on a seeded venue, which §G ruling 1 refuses for the Demo Malls; only the penthouse, which is not in git, could carry one.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, A16): (a), as one shared tool.** The tool is the internal "Test position" tool: FF-7 builds it with "Walk the route"; FF-8 adds "Put me here" (a long press, beyond the venue too). Applied in AC-31.

**Q3 [spec-writer] What the screen shows, and the wording.** PRD §6.10 says "You're outside ⟨venue⟩", but L113 later ruled that anything about where the shopper is is worded suggestively.
- (a) *Recommended.* A card at the top of the map, "You seem to be outside ⟨venue⟩", with "Where are you?" and "Change mall". No dot; Recentre hidden; the map stays browsable. Go's line: "You seem to be outside ⟨venue⟩. Set where you are to get a route." A G4 row records the wording change. (AC-13, AC-19)
- (b) The same layout with the PRD's exact "You're outside ⟨venue⟩". This differs from L113.
- (c) A full-screen state over the map until the shopper is back inside or sets where they are.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, E2): (a).** Applied in AC-13 and AC-19.

**Q4 [spec-writer] When the state starts and ends.**
- (a) *Recommended.* The reported point decides each update: outside means the state starts at once, and the first update inside ends it. There is no new setting. Smoothing noisy live sources is left to P0-13, once Spike 1 measures them. (AC-7)
- (b) It starts at once, but ends only after N updates in a row inside. N is a new `boundary.*` setting with its value from Spike 1. It travels in the public mall list (A3), which changes P0-04 AC-60's test.
- (c) Accuracy-aware: outside only when the whole uncertainty circle is beyond the boundary. A dot just outside would then be snapped, which differs from PRD §6.10's "never snapped" and needs a G4 row.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, E3): (a).** Applied in AC-7 and EC-10; §8 item 2 is "No".

**Q5 [spec-writer] A route is active when the shopper goes outside.**
- (a) *Recommended.* The trip stays and guidance holds: no directions, cues, buzzes, off-route counting, arrival or turning map. The route stays drawn. The sheet shows the line with "Where are you?", spoken once if the voice is on. "Find another way" and "Guide me step by step" are disabled. Back inside, guidance resumes under the normal off-route rule (L80, L110). (AC-22)
- (b) The trip ends, with a line saying why.
- (c) It is treated as an off-route deviation (L80): the wrong-way notice, then "Where are you?".

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, E4): (a).** Applied in AC-22 and EC-16.

**Q6 [spec-writer] A tap refining an approximate dot (P0-08) lands beyond the boundary.** P0-08's spec is written in parallel. Whatever you rule goes into both specs.
- (a) *Recommended.* Refused: the approximate dot stays, and a line says "That spot is outside ⟨venue⟩. Tap inside the map." This matches P0-06 EC-11, which already refuses an outside origin. (AC-30)
- (b) Accepted as a position, so the out-of-venue state shows.
- (c) Ignored silently.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, A24): (a) from FF-8.** In FF-6, before the guard exists, P0-08 EC-15's rule stands (the point is set and routing refuses it as a start). Applied in AC-30 and EC-26.

**Q7 [spec-writer] A held package whose boundary cannot be used.** Publish should never produce one.
- (a) *Recommended.* The mall opens browse-only: every position is declared outside, Go is disabled with "This map can't place you yet. Try again after the next update.", and internal builds log the reason. (AC-32)
- (b) The mall is not opened, and its card says "This mall's map can't be used. Try again later." (like P0-04 EC-9).
- (c) No check: every position is treated as inside.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, E5): (a).** Applied in AC-32 and EC-4.

**Q8 [spec-writer] A live fix outside while a hand-set position is held (from FF-9).** L106 says the first live-confident fix takes over a hand-set position. Read literally, a shopper outside who sets where they are is overruled by the next fix.
- (a) *Recommended.* Only an inside live-confident fix takes over. An outside fix leaves the hand-set position, and the state shows only when the live source is already in use. Risk: a shopper who set a place and then walked out is not told until the live source takes over again inside. (AC-33)
- (b) L106 literally: any live-confident fix takes over, outside included.
- (c) An outside fix asks "Your phone places you outside ⟨venue⟩. Keep the place you set?" with Keep and Use my phone's.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, A8): (a), within A8's wider rule.** A live-confident fix takes over a hand-set or approximate position only if it is inside the boundary and comes after P0-13 has re-seeded its estimate at the hand-set spot. Applied in §2, AC-33 and EC-27.

**Q9 [spec-writer] A scale target for the guard.** None is stated for the boundary's vertex count or the guard's time per update. PRD §6.10 says "within one position cycle", which is at least 1 Hz under §6.9.
- (a) *Recommended.* No target now. FF-8 measures the guard's time per update on the realme, with Aurora's rectangle and a synthetic many-vertex polygon, as provisional figures with no pass or fail (as A6 did for the map).
- (b) Set a target now (you give the number).
- (c) Defer it to FF-10, when drawn polygons exist.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, A5): (a).** Where nothing states a number, measure and record only, no pass or fail. The phone run follows N12's route (§M 5): a self-contained APK sideloaded from Google Drive, with one results file shared by the owner. Applied in §5, §7, EC-5 and §11.

**Q10 [spec-writer] The design choices DC-1 to DC-5 (§10).**
- (a) *Recommended.* Accept all five as written.
- (b) Accept with changes you name.
- (c) Take each one's stated alternative.

**Ruled 2026-10-12 (L165, A7): (a).** All five accepted; DC-5 is also A8's ruling.
